# Minigame 1
## Devlog
If we compare a complete game built in Unity to a book, the Scenes would be like chapters, the GameObjects would be like paragraphs, and the Components would be like the sentences. 
Scenes are where you craft specific levels of the game, and levels are a way to seperate the gameplay into different parts, much like the chapters of a book seperate the plot into parts.
GameObjects are what constitute the Scenes, and each GameObjects serves a certain purpose, relating to a certain aspect of the game. This is like how paragraphs in a book serve to progress the story and are seperated by particular subjects in order to fit the flow of the story.
Finally, Components are what make up GameObjects, and they are what give the GameObjects their functionality. Sentences are what give the paragaphs their meaning by describing what is happening in the story, like how Components will describe and assign traits to the GameObjects they are assigned to.
